Pet Grooming

Professional skills for grooming and caring for pets

6-Month CourseR1,500

CONTENT OUTLINE

  • Coat Care & Maintenance: Brushing, bathing, drying and de-matting for different coat types
  • Styling & Clipping: Breed-appropriate cuts, scissor work and clipper handling
  • Hygiene & Health Checks: Ear, nail, teeth and skin care, and spotting problems early
  • Safe Animal Handling: Calm restraint, stress signals and grooming-table safety
  • Salon Operations: Equipment care, sanitation and client service

COURSE PURPOSE

Our Professional Pet Grooming programme equips aspiring groomers with comprehensive, hands-on knowledge of coat maintenance, styling, hygiene, and safe animal handling techniques. Designed for all experience levels, this course prepares you to work in commercial salons or launch your own grooming business.

Career Opportunities

Graduates can work as professional pet groomers in veterinary clinics, boarding kennels, mobile grooming vans, or establish an independent pet styling salon.
