Pet Business Management

Building and managing successful pet care businesses

6-Month CourseR1,500

CONTENT OUTLINE

  • Business Setup & Regulations: Licensing, insurance, legal requirements, and industry compliance
  • Financial Management: Startup budgeting, pricing structures, invoicing, and revenue planning
  • Marketing & Branding: Social media promotion, local advertising, branding, and client acquisition
  • Operations & Client Relations: Booking platforms, customer retention, and service agreements
  • Staffing & Ethics: Team hiring, safety standards, workplace ethics, and facility management

COURSE PURPOSE

The Pet Business Management programme prepares ambitious entrepreneurs to start, manage, and grow successful pet care enterprises. Covering financial planning, marketing strategies, industry regulations, and operational workflows, this course helps turn a passion for animals into a professional business.

Career Opportunities

Graduates can launch their own grooming, daycare, training, or pet-sitting business, or pursue managerial roles at established veterinary clinics and pet facilities.
