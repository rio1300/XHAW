Canine Obedience

Training dogs through positive reinforcement

6-Month CourseR1,500

CONTENT OUTLINE

  • Positive Reinforcement: Operant conditioning & markers
  • Essential Commands: Sit, Stay, Heel & Recall
  • Behaviour Modification: Leash pulling & reactivity
  • Socialisation Strategies: Managing anxious dogs
  • Owner Coaching Methods: Client transfer techniques

COURSE PURPOSE

The Canine Obedience Training programme provides comprehensive, hands-on instruction in canine behaviour, learning theory, and modern reinforcement techniques. Students learn how to establish effective communication with dogs, teach reliable everyday commands, and coach owners towards lasting results.

Requirements & Prerequisites

  • Age Requirement: Minimum 16 years or older
  • Animal Welfare Interest: Positive care passion
  • Practical Access: Regular dog access for practicals
