Basic Dog Walking

Safe, professional and enjoyable dog walking

6-Week Short CourseR750

CONTENT OUTLINE

  • Leash Handling & Equipment: Selecting appropriate harnesses, leads, and safety gear for various dog sizes
  • Pack Dynamics & Safety: Managing individual dogs and following safe solo and group walking protocols
  • Behavioural Cues on Route: Recognising reactivity, stress triggers, and environmental distractions
  • Emergency Management: Off-leash encounters, sudden injuries, or extreme weather conditions
  • Client & Route Protocols: Route planning and client pickup/drop-off routines

COURSE PURPOSE

The Basic Dog Walking short course equips current and aspiring dog walkers with professional safety standards, pack handling techniques, leash control skills, and canine behaviour management. Learn how to conduct safe, enjoyable walks for every dog in your care.

Who Should Enrol

Ideal for individuals seeking to launch a freelance dog walking service, shelter volunteers, pet sitters, or dog owners wanting stress-free daily walks with their own pets.
