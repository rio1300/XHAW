Puppy Care

Building a healthy and confident foundation for puppies

6-Week Short CourseR750

CONTENT OUTLINE

  • Early Socialisation: Safely introducing puppies to new environments, people, and other pets
  • House & Crate Training: Routine building strategies to establish proper toilet habits
  • Puppy Nutrition: Meal scheduling, dietary requirements, and safe feeding guidelines
  • Health & Vaccinations: Vaccination schedules, parasite control, and recognising early signs of illness
  • Teething & Play: Managing mouthing, chewing behaviour, and providing appropriate mental stimulation

COURSE PURPOSE

The Puppy Care short course provides essential knowledge and practical skills for raising healthy, well-adjusted puppies. Ideal for new pet owners and shelter staff, this course covers early development stages, nutritional guidance, house training, and essential health monitoring.

Who Should Enrol

Perfect for first-time puppy owners, foster carers, adoption co-ordinators, and pet lovers seeking to give young puppies the best possible foundation in life.
