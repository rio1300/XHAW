About us

Welcome to Pawsitive Pet Academy!

At Pawsitive Pet Academy, we believe every dog deserves the chance to learn, grow and become the best version of themselves. We provide friendly and positive dog training designed to help dogs and their owners build a stronger bond.

Our training focuses on rewarding good behaviour and creating a fun, supportive environment where dogs can learn at their own pace. Whether you have a new puppy or an older dog that needs some extra guidance, we are here to help you every step of the way.

Our History

Founded by passion-driven animal trainer Sarah Smith, Pawsitive Pet Academy was established to revolutionize pet care education through force-free, reward-based methodology. Over the years, we have grown from a small local training clinic into a premier academy offering accredited professional qualifications and flexible short courses.

Our Vision

To be a trusted centre of excellence for pet education, where every dog and owner is supported by kind, effective and science-based training.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, practical and affordable courses that build confident pet owners, skilled professionals and happier animals.

Our Values

  • Positive training – reward good behaviour
  • Animal welfare – every pet is treated with respect
  • Education – clear, practical learning
