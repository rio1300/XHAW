Animal Behaviour

Understanding pets through behaviour and communication

6-Month CourseR1,500

CONTENT OUTLINE

  • Canine & Feline Psychology: Understanding cognitive processes, instincts, and developmental milestones
  • Body Language & Signals: Reading subtle facial expressions, postures, and stress signals
  • Addressing Common Issues: Management strategies for separation anxiety, resource guarding, and reactivity
  • Modification Techniques: Desensitisation, counter-conditioning and reward-based methods
  • Environmental Enrichment: Designing stimulating environments to reduce boredom and anxiety

COURSE PURPOSE

The Animal Behaviour programme delves into the psychological and evolutionary factors driving pet actions. Students learn to interpret non-verbal communication, identify behavioural triggers, and apply science-backed techniques to resolve fear, aggression, and anxiety in domestic pets.

Career Opportunities

Graduates can work as animal behaviour consultants, shelter evaluation specialists, veterinary behaviour assistants, or companion animal trainers.
